# Installer Golf
You're trying to download important software, but you must play digital golf to complete the installation.

Click & drag over the golf ball to aim & launch to reach the goal! Complete all 12 holes to finish the installation.

Some windows can be moved, some have multiple pages, and some can be resized. Use them to cross the screen and reach your goal.
